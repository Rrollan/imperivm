# Product

IMPERIVM is a browser card battler in imperial Rome inspired by crypto culture. The primary experience is free training, followed by free multiplayer and optional collecting.

A player selects a ruler and a 30-card deck. Both Treasuries start at 30 HP. Orders grow each turn and limit plays. Immediate spells act at once; delayed edicts enter a visible queue and resolve on the next own turn. Garrisoned fighters trade their attack for resources. The DeFi, NFT, DePIN and meme factions support different combinations.

The catalogue has 99 cards: 49 free base cards and 50 pack-exclusive characters. Five of nine rulers are free; four come from cases. Ownership and rarity limit legal deck copies. Detailed rules are in [mechanics.md](mechanics.md).

Wallet login creates an iDos-backed profile with a nickname and avatar. A remembered server refresh token restores the same account; a fresh signature is needed after expiration, revocation or a new sign-in. Signing in does not move tokens.

Players buy IMP with SOL through Jupiter into Phantom, then explicitly deposit IMP to iDos. Paid five-card packs cost 100,000 IMP; ruler cases cost 900,000 IMP. Paid legendary probability is 0.0001% for the whole five-card pack. Local demo credits and probabilities are independent and have no cash value.

The marketplace reserves owned Item copies in native iDos listings. Another player purchases the lot with deposited IMP. Confirmed completed sales contribute to reference prices. The seller receives game balance and may request withdrawal subject to iDos eligibility and fees. Free starter copies and unverified legacy ownership do not become tradable merely because they appear in a deck.

Live swap, deposit persistence and paid pack opening were confirmed by the owner. Two-wallet settlement and withdrawal still need live verification. Wager PvP is deferred; experimental NFT badges are disabled. [Release status](idos/public-release-20261010.md).
