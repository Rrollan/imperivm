This is IMPERIVM, a browser card battler where imperial Rome meets crypto culture. You can play it directly on iDos Games.

The game includes ninety-nine cards and nine rulers. Five rulers and the starter cards are free. Here I choose the Strategist and customize a thirty-card deck.

I can train against AI, create a private duel with a friend, or search for a random opponent. Training works without connecting a wallet.

The goal is to destroy the enemy Treasury. Orders limit what I can play. I deploy a fighter, end my turn, and attack when it becomes ready. The targeting arrow guides my attack, and the board shows the damage.

Each ruler has a special ability. Here, my Strategist spends two orders to summon an extra fighter, giving me another tactical option.

Solana powers wallet login and the mainnet IMP token. My profile has a nickname and avatar. Phantom holdings and the deposited game balance are separate. Login alone does not transfer tokens. I tested a real deposit: the balance remained after reloading.

Players can exchange SOL for IMP through Jupiter, then deposit tokens into the game. Five-card packs cost one hundred thousand IMP. The marketplace includes buying, selling, and trade history.

IMPERIVM is available to play now on iDos Games. The current source code is on GitHub in the rebirth branch.
